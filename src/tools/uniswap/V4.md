The actual event ends in int24 tick, uint24 fee. The proposed fee-before-tick order would produce a different topic. toEventSelector on the pinned signature gives 0x40e9cecb9f5f1f1c5b9c97dec2917b7ee92e57ba5563708daca94dd84ad7112f, verified against the real receipt fixture. [Pinned IPoolManager event](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/59d3ecf53afa9264a16bba0e38f4c5d2231f80bc/src/interfaces/IPoolManager.sol#L91)

The emitted deltas have caller debt/credit signs: negative input, positive output. Pool.swap accumulates positive output and subtracts the consumed exact-input amount; PoolManager emits that BalanceDelta, then applies the hook's adjustment and accounts the final caller delta. DeltaResolver negates negative debt for settlement and takes positive credit. The interface's short description of a pool balance delta is insufficient to infer the sign. [Pool arithmetic](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/59d3ecf53afa9264a16bba0e38f4c5d2231f80bc/src/libraries/Pool.sol#L373), [delta orientation](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/59d3ecf53afa9264a16bba0e38f4c5d2231f80bc/src/libraries/Pool.sol#L452)

| zeroForOne | amount0 | amount1 | Executed pool input | Executed pool output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| true | negative | positive | -amount0 | amount1 |
| false | positive | negative | -amount1 | amount0 |
| either | reversed signs or zero | reversed signs or zero | Refuse native inference | Refuse native inference |

PoolManager emits Swap before afterSwap. For exact input, AFTER_SWAP_RETURNS_DELTA adjusts the unspecified output; BEFORE_SWAP_RETURNS_DELTA can adjust either leg. The sampled hook mask 0x2044 means BEFORE_INITIALIZE, AFTER_SWAP and AFTER_SWAP_RETURNS_DELTA. [Emission ordering](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/59d3ecf53afa9264a16bba0e38f4c5d2231f80bc/src/PoolManager.sol#L198), [hook adjustment](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/59d3ecf53afa9264a16bba0e38f4c5d2231f80bc/src/libraries/Hooks.sol#L284)

TAKE/TAKE_ALL pay the router's credit to the bound recipient. PoolManager.take updates transient accounting and calls Currency.transfer; neither emits a native-payment log. ERC-6909 Transfer events represent claims, transfers, minting and burning, and cannot evidence an ETH payment or refund. This is why a direct hooked native output remains pending. [take](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/59d3ecf53afa9264a16bba0e38f4c5d2231f80bc/src/PoolManager.sol#L291), [native transfer](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/59d3ecf53afa9264a16bba0e38f4c5d2231f80bc/src/types/Currency.sol#L40), [ERC-6909 claims](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/59d3ecf53afa9264a16bba0e38f4c5d2231f80bc/src/ERC6909.sol#L79)
