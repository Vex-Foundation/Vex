## Draft declaration

### Virtuals Protocol agent-token intelligence

Virtuals is a read-only research surface for virtuals agents and agent tokens on Robinhood Chain 4663, Base, Solana, and Ethereum. Use it to screen robinhood agent tokens or agents on another supported chain, inspect one agent in depth, follow recent graduations, or browse launch events. It never quotes or executes a purchase, sale, or launch.

**Read:** Compare agents by market cap, volume, newest listing, or recent graduation. Inspect a profile for holder count, concentration, launch context, bounded tokenomics, lifecycle status, the anti-sniper buy-tax window, and the exact venue that trades the token. Use the just graduated view or graduation feed to find agents that moved from bonding-curve pre-graduation into a locked liquidity pool. Use the genesis calendar for upcoming or past genesis sales and the associated launch schedule.

**Quote:** No quote capability is available here. Research does not establish an executable price, minimum received amount, route, or transaction.

**Act:** No action capability is available here. Acquiring an agent token is a separate swap task on the venue named by the research result. Obtain and execute a fresh quote there under that venue's normal controls.

**Limits and judgment:** A pre-graduation agent can be illiquid, lacks a locked liquidity pool, and may never graduate. A verification badge means anti-impersonation only; it is not a quality or safety signal. Before any proposed buy, inspect the anti-sniper state and honor the separately preserved sniper-protection rule. Ranking by newest or graduation does not provide a freshness guarantee, and this declaration makes no cost, quota, or rate-limit promise. Genesis entries are calendar intelligence, mostly on Base; suspicious far-future dates may be filtered as spam, and no participation action is exposed.
